The Hands Behind the Craft

Our Artisans

Every AuraNest piece begins with a maker — a craftsperson keeping India's living traditions alive.

We don't manufacture. We collaborate.

Across India, skilled artisans carry folk-art knowledge passed down through generations. We partner with them directly — honouring their craft, their time, and the imperfect beauty of the handmade. When you bring home an AuraNest piece, you carry a part of their story with it.
