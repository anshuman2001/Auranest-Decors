Our Story

Feel the Beauty
of Home

Bringing India's artisanal heritage into modern homes — one handcrafted piece at a time.

"Every piece we make carries the quiet imprint of the hands that shaped it — and the soul of the home it's destined for."

Rooted in heritage

AuraNest Decors began with a simple belief: that the beauty of a home lies in the stories its objects tell. We work directly with skilled Indian artisans who have carried folk-art traditions — Madhubani, Warli, tribal and beyond — across generations.

From hand-painted terracotta planters to carved woodcraft and serene minimal pieces, each creation is made slowly, deliberately, and with genuine care. No two are ever exactly alike.
